# Topological dynamics of topological groups

*A living, hypertextual encyclopaedia (prototype). Editor: LogicArtificialis.*

This is a prototype of a living, hypertextual reference work on the topological
dynamics of topological groups, with an emphasis on universal minimal flows. It
is meant to grow: entries are added and revised over time. Hover over a
reference to a definition or theorem to preview it.

The same entries are also available in a
[LaTeX version](https://topologicaldynamics.org/latex/), with a permanent tag
for every statement, a dependency graph and a PDF. The
[interactive proof tree](https://topologicaldynamics.org/proof-tree/) draws
every statement with what it rests on; selecting a result shows its
statement, its proof and its proof tree. The
[proof trees of the main results](https://topologicaldynamics.org/proof-tree/main/),
the theorems that no other statement uses, are collected on one page.

% map-of-the-encyclopaedia

**Conventions.** Topological groups and compact spaces are Hausdorff. Actions
are left actions. Conventions on left and right uniform structures differ
across the literature; the ones used here are fixed in {prf:ref}`def-ruc`.

**Sources and contributing.** The encyclopaedia is built from the public
repository <https://github.com/eulogica/topdyn-encyclopaedia>. Every page links
to its source files there, with their history. Corrections and new entries are
proposed by pull request and reviewed by the editor, as explained in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/eulogica/topdyn-encyclopaedia/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

**Status of the seed entries.** The first entries were drafted with the help
of an AI assistant from the cited literature. Statements and attributions were
checked against the original papers' abstracts and statements and against
standard secondary sources, and every bibliographic record was checked against
the publisher or arXiv. They have not yet been reviewed by the editor or by
experts. The entries from [](#sec-kpt-umf) to [](#sec-ea-catalogue) were added
later in the same way, from the full texts of the cited papers where available
and otherwise from their abstracts, and have not been reviewed either. The
entries from [](#sec-maps-transformations) to [](#sec-inductive-limits)
report results from preprints posted on Zenodo, which have
not been refereed. The entries from [](#sec-proofs-maps-transformations) to
[](#sec-proofs-inductive-limits) reproduce the proofs from those preprints, in
the versions cited in the bibliography; the bibliographic data of the works
they cite were checked against Crossref, zbMATH or arXiv.
